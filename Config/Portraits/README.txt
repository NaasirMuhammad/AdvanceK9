ADVANCED K9 CUSTOM PORTRAITS

Place square PNG portraits in this folder. Recommended size: 256x256 or 512x512.

Priority:
1. PortraitFile configured for the K9 profile
2. Profile name, such as rex.png
3. Exact appearance, such as german_shepherd_coat_1_vest_2_texture_0.png
4. Breed or model coat, such as german_shepherd_coat_1.png
5. Model file, such as a_c_shepherd.png
6. Breed file, such as german_shepherd.png
7. default.png
8. Built-in safe breed badge

Original 256x256 portraits for every built-in AdvancedK9 breed and separate German Shepherd, Belgian Malinois and Doberman coat choices are included, including the all-black Malinois. Every bundled image uses the same centered tactical-vest, smoked-background and cyan-ring design. Add-on models and custom textures can use PortraitFile or the exact-appearance naming pattern above.

Portraits are cached. Changing the selected model, breed, coat, vest, vest texture, profile, or PortraitFile refreshes the cache; images are never regenerated every frame.
